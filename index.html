what's hackity?

Hackity 2026 is a fun, high-energy hackathon for high schoolers — beginner friendly, and all are welcome. We're here to build wild ideas, meet new friends, and have a blast doing it.

Fuel up with free boba plus curated lunch and dinner — because great builds need great snacks.

Oh, and there's a Brawl Stars competition too. Code hard, then smash hard.
