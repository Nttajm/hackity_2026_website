what's hackity?

A high-energy day to build wild ideas, meet new friends, and have a blast — beginner friendly and open to all.

so what's a hackathon?

A hackathon is a one-day building sprint. You team up (or fly solo), pick a challenge, and spend the day turning an idea into a real app, game, website, or gadget. At the end, teams demo what they made to judges and the best projects win prizes.

It's not hacking into anything — it's "hacking together" something cool. You'll learn more in one day than in a month of tutorials, and nobody expects it to be perfect.

The day

Draft schedule. Times lock in once the date is set.
  • 8:30 am Check-in · Breakfast snacks food Check in with your team at the stand out front, grab a printed schedule, then head inside. Breakfast snacks are at the check-in desk.
  • 8:30 am Hacking starts hacking Teams begin building right after check-in. Mentors circulate to help. Hacking runs until 8:30 pm.
  • 9:00 am Introduction ceremony Opening remarks, the rules, and a walk through the schedule.
  • 12:30 pm Lunch · Pizza & boba food Pizza and boba in the dining area. More options nearby in the Bay Area.
  • 1:00 pm Brawl Stars tournament games Join the competition in the gaming zone. Prizes for winners.
  • 3:00 pm Afternoon snacks food Snack station restock — grab something quick and keep building.
  • 7:50 pm Judging ceremony Teams present demos to judges at the demo booths. Keep demos short and focused.
  • 8:30 pm Hacking ends · Awards ceremony Hacking wraps at 8:30 pm. Winners announced on stage — take photos and celebrate!
